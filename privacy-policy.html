Shield X Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · applies to Shield X for Android (com.shieldxvpn.app)

Shield X has no accounts and never logs your browsing. The developer receives no personal data and runs no analytics or crash reporting. The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below; Premium has no ads.

Ads (free plan)

Google AdMob shows a banner and optional reward videos (watch a video to unlock Fast servers for a while). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and app information and interactions with ads. This data goes to Google, not to the developer. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in About › Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Premium subscribers see no ads, and their phones make no ad requests.

What leaves your phone, and to whom

  • Server lists. The app downloads the current server lists from the server providers. They see your IP address, as with any web request.
  • City names. The app looks up the city of server addresses at freeipapi.com. That service sees your IP address and the server addresses, nothing else. Skipped while the Tor hop is on.
  • The VPN connection. Your traffic is encrypted between your phone and the server you choose. Servers are run by independent third-party operators. The operator can see your IP address (unless you enable the Tor hop) and any traffic that is not itself encrypted (use HTTPS). Some operators keep connection logs; the log policy is shown for each server. No-log servers keep none.
  • Certificates. Before connecting to a No-log server, the app requests a fresh anonymous certificate from the server provider. No identifier is sent.
  • Leak test (only when you tap it): requests to api.ipify.org and api6.ipify.org, which return the IP address websites see.
  • Fastest-server test (only when you tap "Fastest"): a brief connection to up to 6 servers to measure response time.

Premium subscription

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Shield X checks whether you're subscribed on your device through Google Play's billing service; the developer never receives your payment details, name, email or purchase history, and runs no server.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, favourites, the time left on a Fast-server unlock, optional connection history (off by default; Premium; deleted when you turn it off) and a cached copy of the server lists and city names, in the app's private storage. VPN logs and connection keys are deleted when you disconnect. The app disables Android cloud backup, so nothing is copied to the cloud.

Permissions

  • VPN (VpnService): to create the encrypted tunnel. Used only for the VPN you start.
  • Internet / network state: to connect to servers.
  • Notifications: to show connection status.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on App lock; handled by Android, never seen by the app.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads on the free plan.

Third parties

Apart from Google AdMob on the free plan (above), Shield X contains no third-party tracking code. The third-party server operators, the optional Tor network (via the Orbot app) and the DNS resolver you choose (Quad9, Mullvad or Cloudflare) process your traffic under their own policies.

Children

Shield X is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
